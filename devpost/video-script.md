# Marble Run Sketch: demo video script (final)

Length about 2:01. Voice: ElevenLabs v3 ("Jessica"), light mood tags in [brackets] (not spoken).
Footage: recorded from the Lens running in Lens Studio's Interactive Preview (Sunlit Outdoor scene, No Simulation view). No Specs device was used.
Panel presses and the track drawing are real pointer input in the preview. The close-up chain runs and the bowl-filling shot were triggered by editor-only test settings so the camera could stay close to the action.
Most clips were captured with the Lens slowed 3x and sped back up in the edit, because the preview runs slowly on the laptop used. The placing, Undo and Clear clip was captured at normal speed. Some shots are played slower or faster than real time.
Sound: in-game Lens SFX (drop, goal chime, bell, domino clack, track place, click) added in the edit, timed from the Lens log, plus a locally composed music bed.
